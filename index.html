Frequently Asked Questions

How do I choose a health insurance plan?

Compare coverage, sum insured, waiting periods, exclusions, co-payment, hospital network and premium. Read the latest policy wording before buying.

What is a waiting period?

It is the specified time during which certain illnesses or benefits are not covered, as described in the policy.

Are all treatments covered?

No. Coverage depends on the selected plan, medical necessity, limits, exclusions, waiting periods and policy conditions.

How can I get a premium quotation?

Contact me with your basic enquiry. The final premium and eligibility are confirmed through the insurer's official quotation process.

How can I become an insurance advisor?

Contact me to understand the role, eligibility, training and official appointment process. Appointment is subject to company approval.

Where should I submit a claim?

Use the insurer's official claim intimation and submission channels, and follow the instructions in your policy documents.

Helpful information

Frequently asked questions

General information only. Your policy schedule and wording determine the actual coverage.

What is a pre-existing disease?

A medical condition or illness that existed before the policy's commencement, as defined in the applicable policy wording. Disclose all relevant medical history accurately when applying.

What is a waiting period?

It is a specified period during which certain illnesses or treatments may not be covered. Waiting periods vary by policy and type of condition.

What is cashless hospitalisation?

At an eligible network hospital, the insurer may settle admissible expenses directly with the hospital after authorisation, subject to policy terms, limits and approval.

Can I transfer my existing health policy?

Portability may be available subject to applicable regulations, timelines, underwriting and insurer acceptance. Do not discontinue your existing policy until the transfer is confirmed.

How do I know which plan is suitable?

Consider your age, family needs, medical history, preferred hospitals, budget, sum insured and policy conditions. Request and review the official brochure and policy wording before purchase.

Where can I find official product and claim information?

Visit the Care Health Insurance official website or contact the insurer through the customer support details published on its official channels.
