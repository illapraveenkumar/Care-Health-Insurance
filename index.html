Quick answers

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I choose a health insurance plan?

Compare coverage, sum insured, waiting periods, exclusions, co-payment, hospital network and premium. Read the latest policy wording before buying.

What is a waiting period?

It is the specified time during which certain illnesses or benefits are not covered, as described in the policy.

Are all treatments covered?

No. Coverage depends on the selected plan, medical necessity, limits, exclusions, waiting periods and policy conditions.

How can I get a premium quotation?

Send a basic enquiry using the form below. Final premium and eligibility are confirmed through the insurer’s official quotation process.

How can I become an insurance advisor?

Contact me to understand the role, eligibility, training and official appointment process. Appointment is subject to company approval.

Where should I submit a claim?

Use the insurer’s official claim intimation and submission channels, and follow the instructions in your policy documents.
