FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I select a health insurance plan?

Compare sum insured, coverage, waiting periods, exclusions, co-payment, room limits, hospital network and premium. Read the latest policy documents before buying.

What is a waiting period?

A waiting period is a specified period during which certain illnesses or benefits are not covered, as explained in the policy wording.

Are all medical expenses covered?

No. Coverage depends on the selected plan, medical necessity, limits, exclusions, waiting periods and policy conditions.

How can I request a premium quotation?

Send an enquiry through the form below. Final premium and eligibility are confirmed through the insurer’s official quotation process.

How can I become an insurance advisor?

Contact me to understand role requirements, eligibility, training and the official appointment process. Appointment is subject to company approval.

Where can I check claim status?

Use the official insurer customer service channels and keep your policy and claim reference details ready.
