Helpful information

Frequently Asked Questions

General information only. Your policy schedule, wording and insurer confirmation always take precedence.

How do I choose a suitable health insurance plan?

Consider the people to be insured, age, medical history, preferred sum insured, budget, waiting periods, exclusions, co-payment and hospital network. Review the latest brochure and policy wording before purchase.

What is a cashless hospital?

A cashless facility may be available at an insurer network hospital after the required pre-authorisation and subject to policy terms, coverage, exclusions and approval. Check the current hospital list before admission.

How can I find Care Health Insurance network hospitals?

Use the official hospital locator linked on this website. Hospital empanelment and cashless availability can change; confirm directly with the insurer and hospital.

Are pre-existing diseases covered immediately?

Coverage for pre-existing diseases is governed by the specific plan’s waiting period, underwriting decision, disclosures and policy wording. Do not assume immediate coverage unless confirmed in writing by the insurer.

How do I make a claim?

Inform the insurer within the timelines stated in your policy and follow the official cashless or reimbursement process. Keep medical records, bills and required documents. Use the insurer’s official claims channels for claim status.

Can I renew my policy every year?

Renewal is subject to the policy terms and timely premium payment. Review the renewal notice, updated terms and premium before the due date.

Can I get tax benefits on health insurance premiums?

Tax treatment depends on prevailing tax law, the taxpayer’s circumstances and the tax regime selected. Consult a qualified tax professional for advice.
